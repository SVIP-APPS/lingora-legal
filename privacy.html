Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Lingora ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Lingora iOS app (the "App"). The App has no accounts and no servers of ours: it works directly on your device and keeps as little data as possible.

Information processed to provide translations

  • Text you translate. Text you type or paste, text recognized from your speech, and text recognized in photos is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (Google LLC) to produce the translation. The translation is returned to your device. We do not operate any server in between and never receive this text.
  • Speech. Speech is converted to text by Apple's Speech framework, on your device when supported, otherwise by Apple's speech service under Apple's Privacy Policy. Audio recordings are never sent to us or to Google.
  • Photos. Text in camera captures and images you pick is recognized on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Images never leave your device; only the recognized text is sent for translation.

Google Gemini

Only after you agree in the App, the text to translate and the language pair are sent to Google's Gemini API. The request contains no name, email, location, or account information. Google may process and temporarily retain this text and, because the App uses Google's free tier, may use it to improve its products; see Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. Please do not translate sensitive personal information. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the App under Settings > Allow AI translation.

What is stored on your device

  • Your translation history and preferences are stored only on your device. You can delete history in Settings > Translation history, or by deleting the App.
  • To enforce the free daily allowance, the App keeps a daily counter in your device's keychain. It is never sent to us.

Tracking and advertising

The App contains no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or keep personal data or the content you translate, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete. If you have a request or question, including under the CCPA or GDPR, contact us at support@lingora.app.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
