FAQ

Is Lingora free?
Yes. Every device gets a free daily allowance of translations. When it runs out, come back the next day.

Voice input doesn't work.
Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Lingora in the iOS Settings app. Some languages support text and camera translation but not voice input.

The camera can't find any text.
Hold the phone steady, move closer, and make sure the text is well lit. You can also pick a photo from your library.

How do I delete my history?
Open Settings in the App > Translation history > Clear.
